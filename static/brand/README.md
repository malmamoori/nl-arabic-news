## Final logo prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: production logo for an Arabic-language Netherlands news website named "NL بالعربي".
Primary request: Design one exceptionally polished, distinctive editorial logo. A compact custom uppercase NL monogram in warm Dutch terracotta orange (#d95d39) at the LEFT, paired with the Arabic word "بالعربي" in deep near-black teal (#1c2524) at the RIGHT, reading as the brand NL بالعربي. The Latin monogram and Arabic word must feel designed together and optically balanced.
Design direction: Modern independent newspaper masthead, thoughtful and authoritative, approachable for Arabic readers. The NL should have bold sculptural geometric letterforms, a sophisticated strong diagonal in the N, with one restrained angular cut suggesting a folded news page or a speech corner. Clearly recognizable capital N and L, not abstract illegible initials. Arabic lettering should be beautifully drawn contemporary bold Arabic, correctly connected, exact spelling بالعربي with correct letter dots. Clean generous proportions and highly legible at a small website header size. No other text or tagline.
Composition/framing: One single horizontal logo lockup centered, tight thoughtful spacing, the artwork occupying roughly 90% of width and 65-75% of height. Wide landscape canvas about 3:1 aspect ratio. Emblem on the left, Arabic lettering on the right. Flat orthographic artwork.
Background: GENUINELY TRANSPARENT ALPHA background. No colored background, no white panel, no checkerboard printed into the image. All negative space including inside letter counters is transparent.
Constraints: Only orange #d95d39 and very dark teal #1c2524 visible in the artwork. Razor-sharp vector-like edges, solid fills, high resolution. Must work on a warm cream website. Unique custom typography. No gradients, shadows, texture, 3D, mockups, frame, flag, windmill, tulip, globe, tiny details, stock news icon, watermark, or extra variants. Deliver the actual isolated logo asset, not a presentation.
